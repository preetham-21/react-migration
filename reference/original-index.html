Meet Go Kinetic

Your home connection. Simplified.

Managing your services has never been easier with the new and improved Go Kinetic. Access smarter tools, faster support, and seamless communication - all in one place.

Features

Wi-Fi Manager

Easily manage your in-home Kinetic network with the click of a button.

  • Change your Wi-Fi name or password
  • Enable guest networks
  • View connected devices
  • Perform speed tests
  • Set up parental controls
  • Pause device access

Service Support

Help and support is only a click away.

  • Track My Tech provides technician details and arrival time
  • Real-time support with live chat or our Digital Assistant
  • Recover usernames and passwords
  • Modify appointment dates and times
  • Check the status of your order or support requests

Payment Center

Take control of your billing anytime, anywhere.

  • View and pay your bill
  • Manage your preferred payment methods
  • Enroll in AutoPay
  • Enable paperless billing
  • View your bill and payment history

Alerts & Notifications

Stay informed and in control with personalized real-time notifications.

  • Receive updates on service outages and restoration times
  • Get reminders for upcoming bill due dates
  • Track the status of your support request in real-time
  • Customize notifications for account updates and activity
  • Stay updated on exclusive offers and promotions

Service Status

Keep Updated And Connected With New Tools That Make Accessing Service And Technician Support Even Easier.

Service Status Map

Keep informed with real-time service updates. Quickly identify and report outages in your area with just a few clicks.

Quick Dispatch

Our network tests pinpoint issues so you don’t have to. Schedule a technician visit at your convenience, directly from the app.

Support Request Tracker

Easily track the status of your support requests in real time. Make updates or reschedule appointments as needed—all from one convenient place.

Contact My Tech

Have questions or need help? Reach out directly to your technician for up to 30 days after a service or support visit.
